# TINA — Tax Information & Navigation Assistant

## Authority-Driven AI for Philippine Taxation, Law, Regulation, Compliance, and Business Governance

TINA (Tax Information & Navigation Assistant) is an authority-driven AI platform designed to help professionals, businesses, government institutions, and taxpayers navigate Philippine taxation, legal requirements, regulatory obligations, and compliance processes using verified and traceable authorities.

Unlike conventional AI assistants that generate answers primarily from model knowledge, TINA is built on an **Authority Lock Architecture** that prioritizes retrieval, verification, and authority ranking before answer generation.

---

## Mission

To become the authoritative AI navigation layer for Philippine taxation, law, regulation, compliance, government services, and business operations.

---

## Core Principles

### Authority First

No verified authority, no authoritative answer.

TINA is designed to prioritize:

* National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC)
* Revenue Regulations (RR)
* Revenue Memorandum Circulars (RMC)
* Revenue Memorandum Orders (RMO)
* Revenue Audit Memorandum Orders (RAMO)
* Republic Acts (RA)
* Supreme Court Decisions
* Court of Tax Appeals Decisions
* Official Government Issuances

before generating legal or tax guidance.

### Retrieval Before Generation

TINA does not treat retrieval as optional.

Every answer follows:

Question
→ Classification
→ Authority Planning
→ Retrieval
→ Verification
→ Authority Ranking
→ Answer Generation

### Authority Lock

TINA prevents:

* Hallucinated citations
* Fake authorities
* Unsupported legal conclusions
* False source promotion
* Unverified regulatory references
