# Legal Effectivity Review

Decision: supported.

RA 12214 approval date: 2025-05-29.

Publication evidence:
- Supreme Court E-Library records: Manila Bulletin, June 04, 2025 p.9.
- Supreme Court E-Library also records: 121 OG No. 23, 5279 (June 9, 2025).

Effectivity clause: Section 29 states that the Act takes effect after fifteen days following completion of publication in the Official Gazette or in at least one newspaper of general circulation. The clause is disjunctive, so the earlier qualifying newspaper publication controls.

Counting: 2025-06-04 + 15 calendar days = 2025-06-19. The effectivity date is inclusive.

Section 28: the July 1, 2025 date applies only to the identified financial-instrument transitory treatment. It does not displace Section 29 general effectivity and does not establish a different Section 51(C)(2) cutover.

Section 51(C)(2) cutover: 2025-06-19.

Unresolved issue: none.
