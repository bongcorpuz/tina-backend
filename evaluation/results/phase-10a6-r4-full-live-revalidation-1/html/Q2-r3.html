The taxpayer argued that the assessment was invalid due to the lack of a proper pre-assessment notice (PAN) and failure to comply with due process requirements. **Issue** The primary issue was whether the BIR's failure to issue a proper PAN rendered the deficiency tax assessment null and void. **Ruling / Holding** The Court of Tax Appeals (CTA) ruled in favor of the taxpayer, stating that the BIR's failure to issue a PAN constituted a violation of the taxpayer's due process rights. Consequently, the assessment was declared null and void. **Doctrine** The ruling established that tax assessments issued without adhering to due process requirements, specifically the issuance of a PAN, are invalid. The government must respect the rights of taxpayers in the assessment process, ensuring that they are given proper notice and an opportunity to respond. **Application** This case underscores the importance of procedural compliance by the BIR in tax assessments. Taxpayers must be afforded their rights to due process, which includes receiving a PAN before any deficiency tax assessment is finalized. The ruling serves as a precedent for similar cases where procedural lapses by the BIR are challenged. **Status / Limits** The decision in Dizon Farms Produce, Inc. v.
