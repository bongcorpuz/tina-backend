Prompt

A VAT-registered supplier sells fresh frozen seafood in its original state. Is the sale VAT-exempt, and is a separate VAT-exemption certificate required, or is the BIR Certificate of Registration sufficient?

Answer

### Short Answer ### Controlling Authorities 2. **NIRC Section 236** - Outlines the registration requirements for VAT-registered persons. ### Interpretation ### Practical Meaning For VAT-registered suppliers selling fresh frozen seafood, they do not need to obtain an additional VAT exemption certificate to validate the VAT-exempt status of their sales. The existing BIR Certificate of Registration is sufficient for compliance and verification purposes. This simplifies the process for suppliers, allowing them to focus on their business operations without the burden of additional documentation for VAT exemption.
