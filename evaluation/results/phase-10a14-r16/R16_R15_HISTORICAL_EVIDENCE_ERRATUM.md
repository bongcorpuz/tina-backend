## 3. The exact commands behind each attempt

Each of the five attempts was one invocation of
`node --env-file=.env evaluation/results/phase-10a14-r15/run-gates.mjs c38a073b…`, and each
such invocation launched **four** runner processes in this order:

1. `node scripts/run-regressions.mjs` (deterministic cycle 1)
2. `node scripts/run-regressions.mjs` (deterministic cycle 2)
3. `node scripts/run-staging-smokes.mjs` (staging cycle 1)
4. `node scripts/run-staging-smokes.mjs` (staging cycle 2)

Verified: every one of the five journal generations contains exactly 4 attempt
directories, and every one of the five evidence directories contains exactly 4 preserved
logs. **Total actual runner launches across R15: 20** — 10 deterministic and 10 staging.

## 4. What the formal count omitted, and why the two numbers differ

Neither number was fabricated; they measure **different things and neither stated its
scope**:

- **`runnerInvocations: 4`** was written by `run-gates.mjs` at the end of a **single**
  invocation, and describes only the four runner launches of **that one attempt** — in
  practice the last one to complete.
- **"five"** in the R15 narrative counts invocations of the **gate wrapper**
  (`run-gates.mjs`), i.e. gate *attempts*, not runner launches.

So the formal artifact reported a per-attempt figure while the narrative reported an
attempt-count figure, and the same word — "attempt" — was used for both. Under the frozen
R16 counting definitions the correct figures are: **deterministic runner invocations 10,
staging runner invocations 10, gate-wrapper invocations 5.**

This is precisely the defect recorded as P1-R15-IR-005, and it is why R16 derives every
count from one machine-generated registry rather than from any narrative.

## 5. Why R15 reconciliation reported zero technical failures

`R15_ATTEMPT_RECONCILIATION.json` was generated over the **campaign** journal generations
(PREFIX, POSTFIX, FINAL, LIVE). Its `technicalFailures: 0` is true **for that scope**: no
semantic, routing, persistence or live probe ended in a transport or process fault.

The gate-runner attempts were **not** in that scope, and the artifact did not say so. Two
deterministic gate cycles per attempt across attempts 1–5 exited non-zero. Reporting
`technicalFailures: 0` without a scope statement therefore reads as a stronger claim than
the evidence supports. R16 makes a scope statement mandatory in every count summary.

## 6. The R15 pre-fix manifest is stale in the final tree

`evaluation/results/phase-10a14-r15/PRE_FIX_EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.sha256` was generated at
R15 COMMIT 3, when the pre-fix generations existed as ~4,764 per-attempt files. R15 later
converted completed generations to JSONL archives, removing those directories from the
working tree. The manifest still lists the removed paths and therefore no longer validates
against the final tree.

**This file is deliberately NOT modified or regenerated by R16.** Regenerating it would
destroy the record of what R15 actually hashed at that commit.

Which R15 manifests remain valid:

- `evaluation/results/phase-10a14-r15/EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.sha256` — the final R15 manifest,
  reported by R15 as 4,876 entries with 0 missing and 0 hash mismatch;
- the per-generation `*.integrity.json` archive records, which hash-validate the archived
  event content.

## 7. The archive conversion, stated precisely

R15 converted completed journal generations into append-only JSONL archives with
per-event hashes, verified before removal. The **event content** hash-validates.

**No claim is made that the original per-attempt directory form still exists in the final
tree for the converted generations.** It does not. For the two pre-fix generations the
directory form remains recoverable from R15 COMMIT 3 (`4340279`) in git history; for the
six `POSTFIX*-WORKING-TREE` diagnostic generations it does not, because those were never
committed in directory form.

## 8. Historical protected-path violation

During R15 COMMIT 7, `git add -A evaluation/` swept four previously-untracked files under
`evaluation/factcheck/` into a commit. They were removed from tracking with
`git rm --cached` in a following commit, leaving file contents untouched and restoring the
untracked state.

R16 **cannot and does not erase that history**. The violation occurred and is preserved in
git history. R16 asserts only that it has not itself touched, staged or committed that
path, and verifies the final protected state. R16 makes **no** claim that no historical
violation occurred.
