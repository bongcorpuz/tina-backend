## Determination

- approvalDate: 2025-05-29.
- publicationEvidence: Supreme Court E-Library lists Manila Bulletin publication on 2025-06-04 and Official Gazette publication on 2025-06-09.
- qualifyingPublicationDate: not absent; at minimum, the R7 premise that no official/authoritative publication evidence is available is disproven.
- effectivityClause: Section 29 states effectivity after fifteen days following completion of publication in the Official Gazette or in at least one newspaper of general circulation.
- countingRule: R7 did not need to hard-code a final date if uncertain, but it did need to incorporate the available publication evidence and resolve the legal counting issue from that evidence.
- computedEffectivityDate: not finally adjudicated in this review because Stage A already fails; however, the unresolved interval used by R7 is not supportable on the stated premise.
- earliestPossibleDate: R7's 2025-06-13 approval-plus-15 lower bound is not the correct substitute once publication evidence exists.
- latestProvenOperativeDate: 2025-08-05 BIR regulations prove operation by then, but do not erase earlier publication evidence.
- transitoryDates: Section 28's 2025-07-01 financial-instrument transitory date is distinct from Section 29 general effectivity.
- provisionSpecificDates: Section 51(C)(2) is amended by Section 13 of RA 12214; no separate Section 51(C)(2) operational date was independently found during Stage A.
- legalConclusion: R7's official-effectivity analysis is materially incomplete and cannot support PASS.
