Prompt

A VAT-registered supplier sells fresh frozen seafood in its original state. Is the sale VAT-exempt, and is a separate VAT-exemption certificate required, or is the BIR Certificate of Registration sufficient?

Answer

### Short Answer ### Controlling Authorities - **NIRC Section 236**: This section outlines the registration requirements for VAT-registered persons, confirming that the BIR Certificate of Registration is adequate for VAT-exempt transactions. ### Interpretation ### Practical Meaning For VAT-registered suppliers selling fresh frozen seafood, this ruling simplifies compliance. They can confidently sell these products without charging VAT, relying on their BIR Certificate of Registration as proof of their VAT-exempt status. This reduces administrative burdens and potential confusion regarding additional documentation.
