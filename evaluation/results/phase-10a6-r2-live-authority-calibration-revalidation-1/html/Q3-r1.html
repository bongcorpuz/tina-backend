Prompt

A Philippine company paid storage, handling, overtime, and ancillary charges under a contract titled “Contract of Lease.” Which payments are subject to 5% EWT as rent and which may be subject to 2% EWT as services, especially where the contract provides one bundled price?

Answer

### Issue Presented Under the National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC), payments classified as rent, such as storage and handling charges under a lease agreement, are subject to a 5% Expanded Withholding Tax (EWT) as per Section 28(B)(1). Conversely, ancillary charges related to services, such as overtime, may be subject to a 2% EWT as per Section 28(B)(5)(a). The classification depends on the nature of the charges and the terms of the contract. ### Relevant Facts / Assumptions The factual circumstances have not been fully described.
