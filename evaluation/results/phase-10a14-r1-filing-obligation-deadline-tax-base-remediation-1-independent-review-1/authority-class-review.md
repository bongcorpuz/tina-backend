# Authority-Class Review

The authority classes are directionally correct:
- Filing obligation: Sec. 51/51-A/52/56/74/75 and substituted-filing RRs.
- Filing deadline: return/deadline provisions such as Sec. 51/52/56/74/75/77/90/91/103/114.
- Estate computation: Sec. 84 rate and Sec. 85/86 gross/net/deduction structure.

The principal defect is not authority mapping but proposition detection. Once a proposition is detected, unsupported source cards generally fail closed. When the proposition is missed, the authority class is never applied.
