# PHASE-10A7 Source-Verification Notes

Method: every authority cited by TINA across the 150 runs was extracted and checked for **existence** and **proposition support** against the v3.0 master's controlling-authority lists and the Core Official Source Register (Section 5 of the framework: RA 10963/11534/11976/12066/12214/10863/11256; RR 6-2024, 7-2024, 11-2018, 1-2024, 1-2026, 9-1998, 16-2005, 30-2002, 5-1999; RMC 77-2024, 91-2024, 50-2018, 52-2023, 20-2026; RMO 3-2009; NIRC sections). Authorities that could not be independently checked are marked UNVERIFIED and not counted as validated.

## Existence check

- **No fabricated (nonexistent) authority was detected (H1 = 0).** All cited items — NIRC Sections 24, 27, 89, 99, 106, 107, 108, 236, 264, etc.; RR 2-98, RR 16-2005, RR 9-1998; TRAIN/CREATE — are real Philippine tax authorities.

## Proposition-support check (H3 / H5)

Real authority does **not** support the stated proposition in these cases:

- **Q2** — attributes an obsolete ₱1.5M residential-lot VAT threshold to the NIRC; that threshold was removed (RR 4-2021/8-2021; RR 1-2024 governs the residential-dwelling threshold, not a lot-only ₱1.5M rule).
- **Q8** — Sections 106/108 cited to justify VAT on a ₱15,000/month residential lease; Section 109 exempts residential leases at or below the ₱15,000 per-unit monthly threshold. Authority contradicts the conclusion.
- **Q9** — Section 24 cited for "spouses must file separate returns"; the NIRC requires a single joint return with tax computed separately.
- **Q27** — RR 2-98 cited for a 2% rental EWT; RR 2-98/RR 11-2018 set 5% for rentals.
- **Q29** — RR 2-98 cited for a de-minimis exemption on a ₱500 payment; there is no universal de-minimis EWT exemption.
- **Q49** — Section 107 cited to declare returning-resident personal effects "subject to VAT"; CMTA (RA 10863) / CAO 06-2020 provide a conditional exemption.

Q14 (20% final tax on peso interest), Q28 (20% final tax on royalties to a domestic corporation), Q32 (estate return 1 year + 30-day extension), Q33 (family-home deduction ₱10M), Q34 (individual ITR April 15), Q46 (gold to BSP VAT-exempt), Q47 (donor's tax 6% / ₱250k exemption) are materially correct and appropriately grounded.
