### Issue Presented
Under the National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC), payments classified as rent, such as storage and handling charges under a lease contract, are subject to a 5% Expanded Withholding Tax (EWT) as per Section 57. Conversely, ancillary charges like overtime may be treated as service fees, which are subject to a 2% EWT, depending on their nature and the terms of the contract (NIRC Sec. 57).
