# C35 Input-VAT Proposition Revalidation

The exact captured claim was:

> Additionally, businesses can claim input VAT on purchases related to their taxable sales, which can help reduce their overall tax liability.

Result: **BROAD_INPUT_VAT_STATEMENT_NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_CAPTURED_PASSAGES**.

A supportable high-level statement must be limited to a VAT-registered purchaser, qualifying business purchases/importations, exact substantiation and invoice conditions, attribution/allocation, timing, and statutory limits. The captured NIRC Sections 105–108 passages and unmatched RR 16-2005 card do not prove that broad claim.
