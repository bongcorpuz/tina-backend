## 1. Controlling strategic decision

TINA will use a two-stage product strategy.

### Stage 1 — Research-First V1

TINA V1 will operate as a specialized Philippine tax research platform with:

- tax questions answered from verified Philippine tax authorities;
- semantic and structured authority search;
- a searchable tax-authority library;
- full-text source reading;
- exact-passage citation verification;
- source download;
- tax summaries and digests;
- basic source-grounded tax memorandum drafting;
- limited secure document or image context;
- user accounts, history, feedback and controlled subscriptions; and
- published, reproducible Philippine tax benchmarks.

### Stage 2 — Full Philippine Tax Operating System

After Research V1 is operating, TINA will continue toward:

- taxpayer and transaction tax mapping;
- deterministic tax computation;
- accounting-to-tax reconciliation;
- professional workpapers;
- preparer-reviewer-approver workflows;
- compliance actions;
- audit-defense records;
- correction and recall;
- ERP and accounting integrations;
- Tax Control APIs;
- the Philippine Tax Graph; and
- long-term institutional continuity.

The long-term destination is not reduced. Only the first commercial operating scope is narrowed.

## 2. Product benchmark and boundary

The V1 benchmark is the operating pattern of a specialized Philippine legal-research platform:

```text
Ask
→ Search
→ Browse
→ Verify
→ Read
→ Cite
→ Summarize
→ Draft
```

TINA’s narrower and differentiated form is:

```text
Ask a Philippine tax question
→ retrieve tax-specific controlling authorities
→ distinguish authority class and effectivity
→ answer with exact citations
→ open and verify passages
→ export sources or a source-grounded draft
```

TINA Research V1 does not need to equal a general legal platform’s total document count, agency breadth, user count or years of operational maturity before launch.

It must instead prove:

- superior Philippine tax specialization;
- transparent coverage boundaries;
- zero invented tax authorities;
- proposition-to-passage traceability;
- correct authority hierarchy;
- correct effectivity treatment;
- clear limitations where the corpus is incomplete; and
- professional usefulness for CPAs, tax practitioners and in-house tax teams.

## 3. Research V1 launch definition

Research V1 is considered operating only when a real authorized user can complete this workflow:

```text
Create and verify an account
→ ask or search a Philippine tax question
→ receive a source-grounded answer
→ open every material citation
→ inspect the exact supporting passage
→ read or download the full authority
→ save the query and answer
→ provide feedback or report a source issue
→ receive a safe limitation when evidence is insufficient
```

A paid user must additionally be able to:

```text
select a plan
→ complete a live payment
→ receive the correct entitlement
→ avoid duplicate charges or provisioning
→ view billing status
→ cancel or obtain support under documented rules
```

## 4. V1 feature scope

### 4.1 Ask

Provide:

- plain-language Philippine tax questions;
- mode-aware tax answers;
- structured issue, rule, application and conclusion where appropriate;
- direct links from material propositions to source passages;
- visible assumptions and missing facts;
- effectivity and supersession warnings;
- authority-class labels;
- source insufficiency and fail-closed behavior; and
- answer history.

### 4.2 Search

Provide:

- semantic search;
- keyword and exact-title search;
- filters by authority class;
- filters by issuing agency;
- filters by tax type;
- filters by date, effectivity and status;
- deterministic ranking controls;
- bounded and paginated results;
- result snippets with highlighted terms; and
- canonical, historical, superseded and pending-review labels.

### 4.3 Tax Authority Library

V1 launch libraries should prioritize:

1. National Internal Revenue Code and amendments;
2. revenue regulations;
3. revenue memorandum circulars;
4. revenue memorandum orders and approved administrative issuances;
5. BIR forms, instructions and filing references;
6. Supreme Court tax jurisprudence;
7. Court of Tax Appeals decisions within the approved corpus;
8. tax treaties;
9. CREATE, incentives and approved FIRB/PEZA materials;
10. selected customs and local-tax authorities relevant to launch benchmarks.

Document count is not the controlling gate.

Launch corpus readiness is determined by:

```text
at least 95% complete-authority coverage across the locked V1 benchmark
100% coverage for designated critical benchmark classes
0 unreviewed documents presented as canonical truth
0 broken material citation targets
```

### 4.4 Reader and citation verification

Provide:

- full-text reader;
- exact-passage highlighting;
- source metadata;
- authority status;
- issue, effectivity and supersession metadata;
- page or section provenance where available;
- citation-ready copying;
- source download;
- report-source-error action; and
- answer-to-proposition-to-passage navigation.

### 4.5 Summaries and drafts

Provide only controlled outputs:

- authority summaries;
- case digests;
- issue outlines;
- source-grounded research memoranda;
- tax-position draft sections;
- document-request lists; and
- citation packages.

Drafts must:

- remain linked to sources;
- show assumptions;
- distinguish source statements from analysis;
- carry appropriate professional-review notices; and
- never represent themselves as approved filing, legal opinion or final professional advice.

### 4.6 Limited uploads

Research V1 may accept:

- images;
- text-searchable PDFs;
- short source excerpts; and
- limited document context.

Uploads are evidence or context, not automatically legal authority.

V1 does not require full spreadsheet, Word, large-batch OCR or enterprise document-management capability.

2. Research V1 is not the full Tax Operating System.
3. TINA may match the research workflow without matching a general legal platform’s corpus size.
4. Launch corpus quality and benchmark coverage control; raw document count does not.
5. Every material proposition must link to an exact supporting passage.
6. No invented tax authority, acronym, form or effectivity statement.
7. Search and source reading must work independently of chat.
8. Source insufficiency must be visible and must fail closed.
9. Uploaded content is not automatically authoritative.
10. Model output is analysis, not authorization.
11. No autonomous filing or final professional signoff.
12. No public operation without identity, TLS, validation, rate limits, secrets and monitoring.
13. No paid operation without live-payment integrity.
14. No unbounded source, history or account endpoints.
15. No new source becomes canonical without approved governance.
